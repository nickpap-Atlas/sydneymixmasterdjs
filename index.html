About Nick

Sydney's nightlife
in your hands

Nick Pap is a name synonymous with the vibrant pulse of Sydney's nightlife. Founded on the principle that music should move people — really move them — Sydney Mix Master DJs brings over 15 years of expertise to your most important day.

From intimate ceremonies at boutique venues to grand receptions at Le Montage and Doltone House, Nick tailors every set with precision, reading the room in real time to keep every generation on the floor.

Every set is tailored from scratch — no pre-programmed playlists, no autopilot. Nick reads the room in real time, seamlessly blending tracks to keep every generation on the floor from the first song to the last.
